# Girovo 5.4.2

Settings get a page of their own.

## Einstellungen
- **A settings page** – language, appearance, automatic logout, company logos, usage data, what's kept on your computer or phone, and updates, in one calm page instead of the session menu.
- **The gear at the bottom of the navigation rail** opens it on the desktop, "Einstellungen" in the session menu opens it everywhere, and **Strg+,** opens it from the keyboard.
- **Search finds every setting** – type "Sprache", "Logos" or "Gerät" and Girovo takes you straight to it.
- **The session menu is about your session again** – who's logged in, since when, your banks, and Abmelden.
