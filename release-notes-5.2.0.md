# Girovo 5.2.0

Standing orders and scheduled transfers, several banks in one view, your transactions kept on this computer if you want – and budgets, notes and a check of the automatic categories.

## Standing orders and scheduled transfers
- **Right in the transfer form** – under "When", choose "On a date" for a scheduled transfer or "Regularly" for a standing order, within the dates, frequencies and days your bank allows. They appear only where your bank offers them over FinTS; otherwise the form stays as it was.
- **The review says it in plain words** – "First on 01/11/2026, then monthly on the 1st – until further notice", next to exactly what your bank receives.
- **See what your bank holds** – the standing orders and scheduled transfers your bank keeps for an account, at the end of Contracts & subscriptions, read when you ask. Delete one after a confirmation that names exactly what goes.
- **As careful as a transfer** – name check, approval in your banking app and "Status unclear" work the same way. An unclear order or deletion is never sent again by itself; "Check now" only reads your bank's list. The duplicate warning knows standing orders and scheduled transfers too.

## Several banks
- **Log in to another bank** – "Log in to another bank" in the account list or the session menu runs the usual login: bank, login name and PIN, approval. Its accounts appear beside the first ones, grouped by bank.
- **One overview** – one total balance across your banks, and Analysis ("All accounts"), Contracts & subscriptions, search and the transfer form cover all of them. Every order goes through the bank of the account it is sent from.
- **Next time** Girovo offers "Also log in to …" with your login name filled in – you still type the PIN and approve. "Remove bank" forgets it again.
- **If one bank's session ends, only that bank is logged out.** With more than one bank, approvals and messages say which bank is asking.

## Keep transactions on this computer
- **New in the session menu, and off unless you switch it on.** Your transactions are kept encrypted with your PIN and never leave your computer.
- **Analysis, Contracts & subscriptions and the balance history reach further back**, and the period menu has "Everything saved". The list marks where the saved days begin, and the balance history over them is still checked against your bank's balances.
- **Fewer approvals for long periods** – Girovo asks your bank only for what it has not kept yet, and always reads the last 90 days afresh: for every day it delivers, your bank's answer counts.
- **Gone when you say so** – switching it off, deleting your saved data or "Forget device" with deletion removes the saved transactions, every backup with them.

## Budgets, notes and the category check
- **Monthly budgets** for any spending category, in Analysis: what is left, or how far over you are, for the month you are looking at. A month that is not fully loaded says so, and its figures are minimums.
- **Notes on transactions** – add your own line to any transaction. It is marked in the list, found by search and included in the CSV export, but never printed on the receipt.
- **Category check** – how well the automatic categories fit your transactions, which ones you changed, and the ones still without a category, filed in a click. Worked out on your computer, sent nowhere.

## Also
- **Messages from your bank can be saved as a PDF** – a notice like new terms and conditions stays with you after you log out, printed exactly as your bank wrote it.
- **The installer speaks English too** – German on a German Windows, English on any other, without asking.
- **Ctrl++ zooms in again** – on a German keyboard, Ctrl+- made everything smaller but Ctrl++ did not make it bigger again. Now both work on any keyboard, the number pad's + and − too, and Ctrl+0 goes back to normal size.
- **An unexpected error in Girovo itself** is now said in Girovo's own window with "Restart Girovo", like the others, instead of Windows' plain message box.
