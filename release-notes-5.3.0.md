# Girovo 5.3.0

Girovo for Android: your banking on your phone, from the phone straight to your bank – no server in between.

## Girovo for Android
- **What the desktop app does, on your phone** – Übersicht, Umsätze, Analyse, Verträge & Abos, transfers with the payee check, scheduled transfers, standing orders and GiroCode, documents and the Postfach, several banks in one view, and your transactions kept on the phone if you want.
- **Straight to your bank** – the phone talks to your bank over FinTS itself. Nothing goes through a server.
- **Made for the phone** – Material 3 Expressive in your wallpaper's colours (Material You), light or dark as your phone is set. Your balance up top, your transactions right below it.
- **Log in with your fingerprint** (optional) – your PIN is sealed with a key only your fingerprint opens. A new fingerprint on the phone deletes it.
- **Approve in your banking app** – Girovo stays connected while you switch to your bank's app to approve a login or a transfer.
- **Smarter categories as an optional download** – about 300 MB, once; after that it sorts on your phone what the rules don't recognise.
- **Updates** – the APK on this page updates itself, each download checked against GitHub's SHA-256.

Android 8.0 or newer. Material You colours from Android 12.
