# Girovo 5.10.2

## Everywhere
- **Impressum** – Einstellungen → Über Girovo now links the legal notice next to the privacy policy. Girovo is provided by MT-Media; both pages cover every version of the app.

## Android
- **Girovo Plus in the Google Play version** – an optional supporter subscription (0,99 € a month or 9,99 € a year, 3 days free) for several banks, the month-end forecast, "Frag deine Umsätze" and a gold app icon. **The GitHub version stays complete and free:** nothing is locked in this APK.
- **The home-screen icon may disappear once** – Girovo's launcher entry moved (it is what lets Play subscribers switch to the gold icon). Some launchers drop the shortcut with this update; Girovo stays in the app drawer, from where you can put it back.
