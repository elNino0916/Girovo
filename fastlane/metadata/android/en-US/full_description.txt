Girovo connects your phone directly to your bank – over FinTS, the access German banks and savings banks offer for banking apps. No server in between, no account with us: your login and your transactions stay between your phone and your bank.

OVERVIEW
• Balance with its history, available amount, overdraft and pending bookings at a glance
• Transactions with search, filters and categories
• Several banks in one view

TRANSFERS
• Transfers, instant transfers, scheduled transfers and standing orders
• Verification of Payee before the money leaves
• Scan an invoice's GiroCode with the camera – or share the invoice as an image or PDF with Girovo
• Approve in your banking app (e.g. pushTAN): Girovo stays connected while you approve

INSIGHTS
• Income, spending and categories per month
• Girovo finds your contracts and subscriptions in your transactions by itself
• Monthly budgets, savings goals and your own notes on transactions

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Optional fingerprint login – your PIN is encrypted with a key only your fingerprint opens
• Saved data encrypted on your phone
• No ads; usage data only if you agree
• Open source (MIT licence): github.com/elNino0916/Girovo

THIS BUILD
• Built without any of Google's libraries: a GiroCode is scanned with ZXing, an invoice's text is read on your phone with Tesseract
• No Gemini Nano: your transactions are sorted into categories by Girovo's own rules
• Everything is included – Girovo Plus exists in the Google Play build only
• Updates come from your F-Droid repository
• Error reports are always sent to the developer's server, without account data, names, IBANs or amounts
• Company logos come from Brandfetch, only after you agree

Girovo works with banks that offer FinTS (HBCI with PIN/TAN) – almost all Sparkassen, Volks- und Raiffeisenbanken and many other banks in Germany. Girovo is an independent project and not affiliated with any bank. The interface is in German and English.
