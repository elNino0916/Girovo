# Girovo 5.3.1

More of Android in the Android app: scan an invoice's GiroCode with the camera, share invoices with Girovo, pull to refresh – and an updater that works again.

## Installed 5.3.0 from this page?
Its update stops at 0 MB. Download `Girovo-5.3.1-android.apk` from this page once and install it over 5.3.0 – your data stays. From then on Girovo updates itself again.

## Pay an invoice
- **Scan a GiroCode with the camera** – "GiroCode scannen" in Überweisen opens Google's scanner. Girovo needs no camera permission for it and gets only the code's text.
- **Share an invoice with Girovo** – from your Gallery, Files or an e-mail: as an image or a PDF. Girovo opens a transfer with the GiroCode's details. Nothing is sent before you check and approve it.
- **Shortcuts on the app icon** – long-press Girovo for Überweisen, GiroCode scannen and Geld anfordern.

## Made for the phone
- **Pull to refresh** your transactions.
- **Überweisen and Geld anfordern take the whole screen** – one place for every step, without the step bar.
- **The back gesture shows where it goes** – an open panel shrinks with your finger and springs back if you change your mind (Android 14 and newer).
- **A short vibration** when a transfer went through, or didn't.
- **Tablets, foldables and landscape** get a navigation rail on the left.
- **Bigger touch areas** for small buttons, and the layout holds with larger system text.

## Usage data, if you want
- **"Nutzungsdaten teilen"** – as on the desktop: only if you agree, and you can change it anytime in the Sitzung panel. Error reports without account data, amounts or names are sent always.

## Fixes
- **Updates from GitHub no longer stop at 0 MB.**
- **Back closes a panel** even after you tapped its text.
- **The close button stays below the status bar** while the keyboard is open.

## Windows and Linux
- Nothing changes on the desktop in this version.
