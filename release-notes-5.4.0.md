# Girovo 5.4.0

One look on every device, TANs you type yourself, and your documents next to your bookings.

## A new look on Windows and Linux
- **The Android app's design, now on the desktop** – Material 3 Expressive in Girovo's blue: one bar that names the page, a navigation rail on the left with Überweisen on top, and flat, calm pages.
- **The Übersicht in two panes** – on a wide window your balance and transactions on the left, Monatsbilanz, Demnächst fällig and Mitteilungen beside them.
- **The start and update windows match** – the same look and the same typeface as the app.

## Approve with a TAN
- **Besides your banking app, every typed TAN method** – chipTAN optisch (the flicker graphic, adjustable in size and speed), chipTAN QR, photoTAN, chipTAN manuell and smsTAN. Girovo shows what your bank sends, you type the TAN.

## Your documents
- **Receipts on your bookings** – attach an invoice or a till receipt, as an image or a PDF, to any booking.
- **Pay an invoice and keep it** – a transfer started from a GiroCode file can pin that invoice to its booking as soon as the booking arrives.
- **Your bank's own Kontoauszüge** – fetch the statements your bank provides and keep them in Girovo. Under Export → "Auszüge deiner Bank".
- Everything stays on your computer or phone, encrypted with your PIN.

## Your Depot
- **See what it holds** – securities, quantities and prices as your bank reports them, and the Depot's value.

## Clearer everywhere
- **Every screen went through a pass** – what was said twice is said once, buttons say what they do, and empty and error states say what is loaded and what to do next.
- **"Kann eine Freigabe erfordern"** now appears once per section, next to the button that asks your bank.

## Fixes
- **A lost connection while a transfer waits for approval** no longer suggests trying again: Girovo says the outcome is open and helps you check before anything is sent twice.
- **Mitteilungen on a remembered device** – Girovo now says it didn't ask your bank for messages, instead of saying there were none.
- **With a Depot selected**, the Übersicht no longer shows month figures a Depot can't have.
- Updated Next.js, React and Electron.

## Android
- Everything above except the new desktop look – the Android app keeps your wallpaper's colours.
