# Girovo 5.2.2

## A start window
- **You see right away that Girovo is starting** – a small window with the version and what is happening now: getting Girovo ready, opening the window, and after an update, finishing it. It stays for at least two seconds, then the app takes over.
- **When a start takes longer, it says so** – the first start after an update or after restarting the computer often needs more time. After ten seconds the window tells you, instead of leaving you wondering whether the click worked.
- **No more empty window** – Girovo's window now appears once it has drawn, not as a blank frame first.
