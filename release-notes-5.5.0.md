# Girovo 5.5.0

Your own categories, a year's summary for your taxes, a backup you can take with you, and controls that are Material 3 Expressive through and through.

## Your categories
- **Own categories** – add categories such as "Spenden" or "Handwerker" in Einstellungen or straight from a booking, rename them or remove them. They are kept encrypted with your PIN.
- **Split a booking** – share one booking out over several categories ("Aufteilen"). Analyse, Budgets and the export count every part in its own category.

## For your taxes
- **Jahresübersicht für die Steuer** – pick a year, the accounts and the categories, and get a PDF, a CSV, or a ZIP with the receipts you kept. Girovo says when a year is not fully loaded, so the sums are minimums until you load the whole year.

## Looking ahead
- **Kontostand am Monatsende** – an estimate of where your balance lands at the end of the month, from what is still pending, your regular payments, the Daueraufträge and Terminüberweisungen at your bank and your usual spending, with a warning when the account could slip into the overdraft.

## Your data
- **Sicherung** – create an encrypted backup file of your personal data, kept bookings and documents, protected by a password of your choice, and restore it on a new device. Under Einstellungen → Auf diesem Rechner.
- **Import older bookings** – bring in history your bank no longer sends from a CSV (Sparkasse, Volksbanken, ING, Girovo's own export), CAMT or MT940 file. Girovo reads the file first and says what it holds.

## Your bank
- **Kontoauszüge as PDF** – fetch the PDF statements of banks that offer them in that form, next to the existing ones.
- **Change Daueraufträge and Terminüberweisungen** – "Ändern …" on an order at your bank, approved like any transfer. Whether a bank allows it depends on the bank.

## Real Material 3 Expressive controls
- **Every button, field and list is Material's own** – buttons, icon buttons, the selects and their lists, the connected buttons for Sprache and Darstellung, checkboxes, switches, chips, menus, the loading and progress indicators, the navigation bar and the Überweisen button now use the M3E component library, in Girovo's colours and your wallpaper's on Android.

## Fixes
- **"Umsätze auf diesem Rechner behalten"** is no longer lost when your saved settings are read back.
