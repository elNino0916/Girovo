# Girovo 5.1.0

Girovo now speaks English, the session menu is a lot shorter, and your profile can have a picture.

## English
- **Switch any time** – on the login screen (top right) or in the session menu. Without a choice, Girovo follows your Windows language: German on a German Windows, English on any other.
- **Amounts and dates follow the language** – "€1,234.56" and "05/10/2026" in English. When you type an amount in English, "1,000" is a thousand and "1.000" is refused rather than guessed; the review always shows exactly what your bank receives.
- **What your bank sends stays as your bank wrote it** – its messages and answers, a payment reference, a booking text. The CSV export keeps the German Excel format, with English column names.
- **The update window and Girovo's own error messages** speak your language too.

## Session menu
- **Much shorter.** Each setting is one row, the login time and the countdown share one line, and the longer explanations moved into the dialogs they belong to.
- **Language** right in the menu: Deutsch | English.
- **A picture for your profile.** Click your initials in the menu and choose a picture of your own, or one of eleven icons – it shows in the top right. Your picture is made small on your computer and kept with your encrypted data, for each login; it never leaves the computer.

## Errors
- **If Girovo cannot start, or has to close,** it now says so in its own window, with "Restart Girovo", instead of Windows' plain message box.

## Search
- **A day can also be typed as "28/09"**, as well as "28.09.".
