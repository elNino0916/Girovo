Girovo AI in Einstellungen: vier Richtungen

Each direction keeps every setting: Girovo AI on/off, Namen aufräumen, Mit Girovo AI kategorisieren, the model's download, and the privacy sentence word for word. Light shows the page once the model is loaded and Girovo AI is on; dark shows it before the model is loaded (the state in your screenshot). Every direction drops the second "Girovo AI" wordmark, dims a disabled row as a whole, and keeps the violet only on the mark.
