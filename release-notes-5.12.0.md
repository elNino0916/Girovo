# Girovo 5.12.0

On Android, one fingerprint now logs you in to all your banks, and lists scroll without getting caught.

## Android
- **Fingerprint login for further banks** – with "Mit Fingerabdruck anmelden" on, the PINs of the banks you added with "Weitere Bank anmelden" are kept beside your first one, encrypted with a key only your fingerprint opens. One fingerprint at login opens them all, and your further banks log in one after another; approvals stay as each bank asks. A bank you log in with its PIN typed asks once for your fingerprint to be kept as well ("Nicht jetzt" skips it). A PIN the bank refuses, or "Bank entfernen", deletes that bank's PIN; adding a fingerprint to the phone or turning the switch off deletes them all. If fingerprint login is on already, it keeps working, and your further banks join it the next time you log one in.
- **Scrolling** – the category menu and the row of filter chips scroll again when your finger drifts a little: pulling down to refresh only takes over on the page itself. A press no longer stays lit while you scroll.
