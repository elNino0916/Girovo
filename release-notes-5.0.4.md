# Girovo 5.0.4

Categories come from a bigger model, and more kinds of business are recognised.

This update is bigger than usual because it brings the new model. The ones after it are small again.

## Categories
- **A bigger category model.** Girovo now uses multilingual-e5-base, which still runs entirely on your computer. In our tests it filed about four in five of the shops, practices and offices the keyword rules don't know. Now and then it picks a neighbouring category, a Reformhaus under Shopping, say. Correct it once and a rule takes over for that name.
- **More kinds of business recognised:** restaurants by their kind (Trattoria, Steakhaus, Kebab), jewellers and electrical shops, hearing-aid acousticians and joint practices, the Hausgeld of a WEG, sports streaming, long-distance buses and broker deposits.
- **Spelling variants of a name you filed by rule** ("Edeka Markt Schmidt" for "EDEKA Schmidt") now take that rule's category more reliably.
