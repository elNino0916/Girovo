# Girovo 5.8.0

On Android you can now ask Girovo about your Umsätze in your own words – Gemini Nano reads the question, Girovo works out the answer on the phone. The phone check also looks at the running system now, and a smaller, faster app is ready for Android 16's large screens.

## Android
- **Frag deine Umsätze** – at the top of the Analyse, ask "Wie viel ging an Amazon?", "Lebensmittel letzten Monat" or "Wie oft war ich bei McDonalds?". Gemini Nano only reads what you are looking for; Girovo works out the figure from your loaded Umsätze, shows what it searched for, and "Umsätze zeigen" opens exactly the bookings it counted. Nothing leaves the phone, and Gemini Nano never states a figure itself.
- **Questions need Gemini Nano v4** – on a phone with an older model, or without one, the Analyse says "Nicht unterstützt" and why, with a button to update AICore in the Play Store.
- **A closer look at the running system** – besides the boot check by the phone's security hardware, Girovo now notices signs of root on a running system, such as root modules mounted over system files or a redirected hosts file. Such a phone counts as modified: the login warns, and Girovo keeps nothing on it. A root manager app that is merely installed, or a preinstalled app hidden by the phone, does not count.
- **Keeping data anyway** – if you know your rooted phone and want Girovo to save on it regardless, hold "Schließen" in Einstellungen → Gerätesicherheit for 10 seconds. A full-screen warning explains what that means; only after "Ich verstehe das Risiko" can you allow it. The fingerprint login stays off on such a phone, and "Wieder nichts speichern" takes it back.
- **Gemini Nano on more phones** – Girovo tries every model variant AICore offers, so phones that only have some of them, such as a Xiaomi 17 Ultra, now get categories too. When AICore has not started a download after a minute, Einstellungen says so and what usually helps.
- **Half the size** – the app is now optimised and shrunk when it is built: the download drops from 7 MB to under 4 MB.
- **Edge to edge, on every Android** – Girovo draws under the status and navigation bars on older Android versions too, as Android 15 and later already did, and no longer fixes the code scanner to portrait on tablets and foldables.
