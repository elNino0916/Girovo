# Girovo 5.9.0

Smarter categories everywhere, and on Android a "Frag deine Umsätze" that ranks and compares – plus tidy payee names and a search that understands "Tanken im August".

## Everywhere
- **Essen & Trinken** – restaurants, cafés, takeaway and delivery have a category of their own, apart from **Freizeit** (cinema, concerts, sport, games, hotels). "Restaurants im August" no longer brings up a Steam purchase. What Girovo filed itself moves to the right one by itself; whatever you filed or budgeted under Freizeit yourself stays there, and Girovo says so once.
- **Games are Freizeit** – Steam, PlayStation Store, Nintendo eShop, Fanatical, Kinguin and other game shops are filed together; game subscriptions such as Game Pass stay under Abos & Medien.
- **Your filing counts for every spelling** – a payee you filed yourself also decides its other spellings ("HORNBACH BAUMARKT FIL. 0815" like "Hornbach Baumarkt AG"), before any built-in rule.
- **More shops known** – headphones and computer gear (Beyerdynamic, Wooting, Logitech …), travel eSIMs and Discord are filed where they belong.
- **PayPal payouts** – money paid out from your PayPal balance to your account counts as an Umbuchung, not as Shopping.

## Android
- **Rankings** – ask "Wofür gebe ich am meisten aus?" or "Wo kaufe ich am häufigsten ein?": the top five categories or payees, each a tap away from its bookings.
- **Comparisons** – "Mehr als letzten Monat?" or "Tanken diesen vs. letzten Monat": both figures and the difference. A running month is compared with the same days of the last one, so half a month never looks like saving.
- **Payee names by brand** – Gemini Nano turns "PAYPAL *EBAY" into "eBay" and "Deichmann-Schuhe GmbH & Co. Vertriebs KG" into "Deichmann" in the lists, and finds their logos more often. Only names that stand in the bank's own text are used; the booking's details keep the bank's text, and the search finds both.
- **A search that understands you** – in the Umsätze list, "Tanken im August" or "Amazon letzten Monat" become the matching filters when you press search.
