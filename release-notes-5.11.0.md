# Girovo 5.11.0

A new look on Android: calmer and clearer, with three places instead of five and search first. The desktop app keeps its look for now.

## Android
- **Three places** – Übersicht, Zahlungen and Analyse in the navigation bar. Zahlungen gathers Überweisen, Geld anfordern, GiroCode scannen, your Vorlagen and the payees you paid last, above Verträge & Abos. "Mehr" and the floating Überweisen button are gone.
- **Search first** – the Übersicht opens with a search bar for Umsätze, payees and the app's actions; a question goes on to "Frag deine Umsätze". The bell moved into the Sitzung panel as Mitteilungen, and a dot on your avatar says when something is new.
- **The balance up front** – large and plain, with Verfügbar and a line of its course; the details open in place. The account's name opens your accounts as a sheet.
- **Transfers start with the amount** – type it on the number pad, then choose the payee, then check everything on the review.
- **Booking details in tabs** – Details, Notiz & Belege and Kategorie; a dot on a tab says a note or a receipt is there.
- **A new login** – the banks' logos as tiles on Girovo's own field, the form on a sheet below it.
- **Type, icons and colour** – quieter type with tidier figures, Material Symbols (filled for the place you are on), Analyse charts in your wallpaper's tones, and calm transitions between places.

## Everywhere
- **Website and contact** – Einstellungen → Über Girovo links girovo.de and contact@girovo.de.
